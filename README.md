# My Favorite Movie: Real Steel
**Real Steel** is a 2011 sci-fi sports drama directed by *Shawn Levy* and starring *Hugh Jackman* and *Dakota Goyo*. Set in the near future, human boxers have been replaced by giant, remote-controlled fighting robots.
---
## Why I Love It
* **Heartwarming Story:** Beyond the robot fights, it is a great story about a father and son reconnecting.
* **Epic Action:** The boxing choreography and CGI look realistic and intense.
* **The Underdog Spirit:** Watching **Atom**, an outdated sparring bot, take on undefeated champions is inspiring.
## Top Robots in the Movie
1. **Atom** – A Generation-2 sparring robot with a rare *"shadow function"* that copies human movements.
2. **Zeus** – The undefeated, state-of-the-art WRB champion built by Tak Mashido.
3. **Noisy Boy** – A flashy, voice-activated manga-inspired robot with powerful moves.
4. **Midas** – A ruthless, gold-and-black street-fighting bot known for breaking the rules.
